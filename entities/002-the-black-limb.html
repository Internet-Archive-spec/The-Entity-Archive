THE BLACK LIMB

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Black Limb is an enormous unidentified oceanic entity observed only during brief surfacing events in deep ocean environments. Investigators have confirmed the existence of a colossal human-like hand capable of dragging virtually any object or organism to the ocean floor. Despite decades of observation, no evidence has revealed what the limb is attached to, or whether the visible hand represents the entirety of the organism or only a single appendage.

PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

The Black Limb manifests as a colossal black human-like hand emerging vertically from the ocean depths. Its appearance resembles little more than a massive silhouette with no distinguishable skin texture, fingernails, or visible anatomical features beyond its overall shape.

The entity most commonly surfaces every few months before retreating once more beneath the ocean. Observations have recorded the limb grasping a wide variety of prey including commercial vessels, oil rigs, whales, schools of fish, and giant cephalopods.

No researcher has successfully observed what lies beyond the wrist. The source of the limb remains completely unknown.

OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

The Black Limb appears to remain motionless within the deepest regions of the ocean until prey enters its range. Because the limb blends almost perfectly with the surrounding darkness below the photic zone, it is effectively invisible until it begins ascending toward the surface.

When multiple targets are present, the entity has repeatedly demonstrated the ability to generate enormous oceanic vortexes. These vortexes gather vessels, marine life, and floating debris into a concentrated area before the limb attempts to retrieve them simultaneously.

After completing a retrieval—or when unable to secure prey before its surface limit is reached—the limb immediately descends back into the abyss. On several occasions, sonar readings have suggested it continues retreating beneath the ocean floor itself, making any attempt to locate its origin impossible.

ABILITIES

  • Extreme Retrieval Strength: The Black Limb is capable of dragging any single object or organism it successfully grasps to the ocean floor regardless of its size, weight, or whether it is living or non-living.
  • Vortex Generation: After surfacing, the entity can generate massive oceanic vortexes that gather numerous organisms and objects into one location, allowing multiple targets to be retrieved during a single surfacing event.
  • Deep Ocean Camouflage: The limb blends seamlessly with the darkness of the abyss, remaining undetectable until it enters water illuminated by natural light.
  • Seafloor Retreat: The entity is capable of descending beneath the ocean floor after completing an encounter, preventing investigators from tracing its origin.

LIMITATIONS

  • The Black Limb cannot remain above the ocean surface for longer than approximately three minutes before retreating.
  • The entity surfaces at a relatively slow rate. Fast-moving or highly agile targets are frequently able to escape before the limb can successfully grasp them.
