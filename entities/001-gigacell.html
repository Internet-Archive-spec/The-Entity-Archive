EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GigaCell is an atmospheric entity believed to embody the destructive force of an EF5 tornado. Rather than existing as a biological organism, its entire form is composed of storm systems and atmospheric energy. Encounters with GigaCell are consistently associated with severe weather outbreaks, including multiple tornadoes and widespread lightning activity. Due to the scale of destruction surrounding its appearances, Bureau protocol limits interaction to observation and documentation.

PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

GigaCell resembles a large wolf-like entity, though no biological tissue has ever been observed. Its head, torso, and limbs are formed entirely from storm clouds. Its eyes appear as brilliant streaks of lightning, while what resembles fur is actually dozens of swirling storm cells continuously flowing throughout its body. The entity can appear roughly the size of a normal gray wolf or expand until it becomes an enormous shape moving through the clouds.

OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

Whenever GigaCell manifests, severe weather rapidly develops throughout the surrounding region. Multiple tornadoes commonly form around the entity, often described by eyewitnesses as "twins." While immensely destructive, observations suggest the damage caused by GigaCell is largely indiscriminate rather than intentionally directed toward specific targets.

ABILITIES

  • Generates extreme weather systems.
  • Frequently creates multiple tornadoes during manifestations.
  • Can freely alter its size from wolf-sized to an enormous atmospheric figure.
  • Can blend into existing storm systems when at its largest size.
  • Maintains precise control over normal lightning strikes and concentrated lightning bursts.

LIMITATIONS

  • Extreme cold significantly weakens the entity.
  • Its greatest displays of power can only be sustained for a limited time.
  • After exhausting itself, the surrounding storm system gradually dissipates.
